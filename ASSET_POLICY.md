# Asset rights policy

Every image, audio file, video file, font, screenplay excerpt and other non-code asset must have a documented publication basis before this repository becomes public.

For each asset or coherent asset set, record:

- repository path;
- title or short description;
- creator or rights holder;
- source;
- date added;
- applicable licence or written permission;
- required attribution;
- whether AI-assisted or AI-generated tools were used; and
- whether a recognizable real person or private location appears.

Do not assume that possession, generation by a tool, availability on the web or inclusion in an older private repository grants redistribution rights.

Assets with unknown ownership, incompatible terms, personal information or unresolved likeness/privacy concerns must be removed from the public branch or kept in a separate private archive. Generated derivatives must not be published when the source asset could not lawfully be used for that purpose.
